# Focuswilds

*A little focus. A little care. A place for cats to call home.*

A big city, a small apartment, and a stray cat that slowly learns to trust you. In Focuswilds, the wilds are the concrete jungle outside your door. This personal focus game turns time spent on what matters into a welcoming home for cats.

Focused time will earn money, player experience and items, while advancing imagined walks through city neighborhoods. Explore alleys, convenience-store corners, bus stops and small parks between buildings; no real-world walking is required. Meet stray cats, get to know them over several encounters, and bring them back to your apartment when they trust you and there is room. Cats include mixed breeds and familiar coat patterns as well as named breeds; every little friend belongs.

At home, care and play build affection. Content cats may offer helpful bonuses; a grumpy cat may offer less extra help until cared for again. Your basic focus rewards remain yours. Care follows the rounds you play, never the hours you spend away.

Buy supplies, sell spare finds, and furnish your apartment with toys, beds, cardboard boxes and bowls. Improve the space to welcome more cats. Each cat has a personality, preferences and a story that begins with where you met. Warm pixel art pairs cool morning streets, shop lights and rain-washed pavement with the soft light of a room that gradually becomes a shared home.

Designed for one player's phone, the experience uses a portrait layout, showing a portrait-mode notice on landscape or square screens. An in-game keyboard is planned for future editing screens.

Sign in and stay signed in on your personal device. The current version contains only the login screen. The city and apartment are the planned setting; focus, city walks, cat care and the shop will arrive one screen at a time.
